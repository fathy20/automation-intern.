EmailJS Configuration

💡 Need a free EmailJS Account?
  1. Register at emailjs.com.
  2. Link your Gmail account in "Email Services" to get a Service ID.
  3. Create an "Email Template" with variables:
    {{subject}}, {{html_body}}, {{plain_body}}, {{to_email}}.
  4. Set the "To Email" field inside the EmailJS Template settings to {{to_email}}.
  5. Copy your Public Key from Account Settings.
